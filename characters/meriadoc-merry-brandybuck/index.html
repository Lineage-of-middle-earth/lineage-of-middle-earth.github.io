Meriadoc 'Merry' Brandybuck

of the Fellowship · Master of Buckland · b. 1382 S.R.

  • Hobbits
  • ★ Key character

Meriadoc "Merry" Brandybuck was a Hobbit of the Shire and one of Frodo Baggins cousins and closest friends. He loved boats and ponies and had a great interest in the maps of Middle-earth. He was also one of the nine companions of the The Fellowship of the Ring, and later became the eighth Master of Buckland.
