Samwise Gamgee

Ring-bearer · Mayor of the Shire · b. 1380 S.R.

  • Hobbits
  • ★ Key character

Samwise Gamgee, also known as Samwise Gardner or simply as Sam, was a hobbit of The Shire. He was Frodo Baggins' gardener and best friend. Sam proved himself to be Frodo's closest and most dependable companion, the most loyal of the Fellowship of the Ring, and played a critical role in protecting Frodo and destroying the One Ring.
