Ori

kept the Book of Mazarbul

  • Dwarves

Ori was a member of Thorin's Company of Dwarves who participated in the Quest of Erebor and the Battle of Five Armies, which he survived. He and his brothers, Dori and Nori, were remote kinsmen of Thorin II Oakenshield.
