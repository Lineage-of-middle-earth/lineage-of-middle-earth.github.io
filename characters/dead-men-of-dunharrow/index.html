Dead Men of Dunharrow

the Oathbreakers · bound to the Paths of the Dead

  • Species & Monsters
  • ★ Key character

The Dead Men of Dunharrow were Men of the White Mountains who broke their oath to Isildur and were cursed to remain undead. Aragorn called them to fight at Pelargir and then released them.
