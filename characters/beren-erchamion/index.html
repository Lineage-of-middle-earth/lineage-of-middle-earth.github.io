Beren Erchamion

Man of the House of Bëor

  • Half-elven

Beren, also called Beren Erchamion, was a man of the First House of the Edain, and one time Lord of Dorthonion. He was a hero whose romance with the Elf-maiden Lúthien was one of the great stories of the Elder Days told for many ages after.
