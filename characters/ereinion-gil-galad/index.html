Ereinion Gil-galad

last High King of the Noldor

  • Elves
  • ★ Key character

Ereinion Gil-galad, born Artanáro, was a Ñoldorin Elf and son to Orodreth. He was the last Ñoldóran in Middle-earth, and bore many titles, including High King of the Elves of the West, King of the Eldar (to some), King of Lindon, Lord of the High Elves (largely because most of them remaining are Ñoldor), and the Lord of Eriador.
