Aredhel

the White Lady

  • Elves

Aredhel, also known as Aredhel Ar-Feiniel, the "White Lady of the Ñoldor", was the only daughter and third child of Ñoldorin High-prince Fingolfin and his wife, Anairë. Aredhel was the sister of Fingon, Turgon, and Argon, and the mother of Maeglin.
