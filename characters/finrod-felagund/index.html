Finrod Felagund

King of Nargothrond

  • Elves

Finrod, known fully as Finrod Felagund, was a Ñoldor Elf who was born in Aman. He was King of Nargothrond and the eldest son of Finarfin and Eärwen, and the brother of Galadriel, Angrod and Aegnor. Finrod was one of the noblest Elves of the House of Finarfin and a friend of Men.
