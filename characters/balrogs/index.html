Balrogs

Maiar of fire corrupted by Morgoth

  • Species & Monsters
  • ★ Key character

Balrogs were Maiar of fire corrupted by Morgoth, wreathed in flame and shadow and wielding whips of fire. Most perished in the War of Wrath, but one remained in Khazad-dûm.
