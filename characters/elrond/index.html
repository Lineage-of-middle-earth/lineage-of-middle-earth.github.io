Elrond

Lord of Rivendell · bearer of Vilya · envoy of Gil-galad · friend of Durin IV

  • Half-elven
  • Rings of Power
  • ★ Key character

Elrond was a mighty Half-elf ruler who dwelt in Middle-earth from the First Age to the beginning of the Fourth Age. He was the Lord of Rivendell and father of Elladan, Elrohir, and Arwen Undómiel, the eventual wife of Aragorn II. He was given the epithet of Peredhel, Sindarin for Half-elven.
