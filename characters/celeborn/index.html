Celeborn

Lord of Lothlórien

  • Elves
  • ★ Key character

Celeborn was the Lord of Lothlórien and husband of Galadriel, Lady of the Golden Wood. He was said to be one of the wisest Elves in Middle-earth at the end of the Third Age.
