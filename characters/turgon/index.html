Turgon

King of Gondolin

  • Elves

Turgon was a Ñoldor Elf of Gondolin, second son of Fingolfin, brother of Fingon, Aredhel, Argon, and father of Idril. In Middle-earth, Turgon was the King of Gondolin and became the High King of the Ñoldor after the death of his older brother Fingon. For centuries of the late First Age, Turgon remained successfully hidden from Morgoth until a betrayal by Maeglin, one of his subjects, caused its downfall.
