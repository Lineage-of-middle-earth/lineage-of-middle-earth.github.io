Tom Bombadil

Master of wood, water and hill

  • Rings of Power
  • ★ Key character

Tom Bombadil was a mysterious entity that lived for much of the world's history, known in the Third Age to dwell in a valley of the Withywindle in the depths of the Old Forest, east of Buckland, and close to the dangerous Barrow-downs.
