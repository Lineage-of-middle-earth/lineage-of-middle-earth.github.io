Eärendil the Mariner

bears the Silmaril as a star

  • Half-elven

Eärendil (Quenya; IPA:) was a great Half-elf mariner who voyaged to Valinor, entreated before the Valar on behalf of the Children of Ilúvatar, and carried a star across the sky at the end of the First Age. His acts had been prophesied of among the Elves centuries beforehand.
