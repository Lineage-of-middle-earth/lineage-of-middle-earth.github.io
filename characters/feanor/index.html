Fëanor

son of Míriel · maker of the Silmarils

  • Elves

Fëanor was a Ñoldorin Elf, second King of the Noldor. It was he who led his people from Valinor in pursuit of Morgoth after the latter destroyed the Two Trees of Valinor and stole the Silmarils.
