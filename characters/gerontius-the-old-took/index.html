Gerontius 'the Old Took'

Thain · 1190–1320 S.R. · lived 130 years

  • Hobbits

Gerontius Took, more commonly known as the Old Took, was a Hobbit of the Shire, and the twenty-sixth Shire-thain. He was best known for being the third oldest hobbit in Middle-earth's history, living to the advanced age of 130 years. Only his grandson Bilbo Baggins and the Stoor Sméagol, whose lifetimes were prolonged by the powers of the One Ring, lived longer.
