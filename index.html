Compiled from Tolkien's published texts, chiefly the Appendices to The Lord of the Rings and The Silmarillion. Where Tolkien changed his mind (for example Gil-galad's parentage) the published reading is shown and the alternative noted. Long royal lines are abridged where marked; every person named in the novels' family trees for the main characters is included.
