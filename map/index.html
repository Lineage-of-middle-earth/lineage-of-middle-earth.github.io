The Third Age

Map of Middle-earth

The lands of the Third Age, from Forlindon and the Grey Havens in the west to Rhûn and Khand in the east, and from the Grey Mountains down to Harad and Umbar. Scroll or pinch to zoom: zoomed out you see the regions, and zooming in brings up cities, havens and ruins. Drag to pan, and tap a region or a place to read about it.

An illustrated map, not Tolkien's own, so coastlines and distances are approximate. It also shows Aman in the far West and the island of Númenor.
